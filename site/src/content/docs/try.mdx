Shared Rust views, running in your browser. Choose a demo to open its interactive canvas.

## [Widget Factory](/widget-factory/)

Controls, text editing, menus and a 3D canvas.
[Source](https://github.com/petitstrawberry/scarlet-ui/tree/main/examples/widget-factory)

## [Animation Lab](/animation-lab/)

One place for motion experiments. **Play** combines a spinning loader, a zigzag
gauge, a bouncing jelly and confetti. **Basics** explores animated view properties;
**Layout** moves between arrangements.

Try **Load again**, **Celebrate!** and **Mix colors**, then change the speed,
pause the motion or reset the scene.
[Source](https://github.com/petitstrawberry/scarlet-ui/tree/main/examples/animation-lab)
· [Animation guide](/reference/animations/)
